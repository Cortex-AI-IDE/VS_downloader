What is Video Saver?

Video Saver is an Android application that saves videos from social media and video sites to your own device. It is written in Kotlin, uses Material 3, and runs the open source download engine yt-dlp locally with a bundled Python runtime. Everything happens on the phone: the app never uploads your links, never asks for a password, and has no advertising or analytics library. Files land in the public Download/VideoSaver folder, where the gallery and any file manager can open them.

Features

Built for one job, done well.

One tap downloads

Copy a link in any app, paste it, tap Download. The platform badge confirms the site before anything starts.

Always H.264 MP4

Downloads prefer H.264 video inside an MP4 container, so the file plays everywhere and uploads to social apps without a decoding error.

Two at a time

A fixed thread pool runs two downloads in parallel and queues the rest, with live percentage and a cancel button.

Survives the background

A foreground service with a progress notification keeps the download alive when you switch apps or lock the screen.

Your files, your folder

Saved to Download/VideoSaver through MediaStore on Android 10 and newer, with open, share and delete actions in the app.

Self updating engine

Sites change often. The header action updates yt-dlp without waiting for a new APK release.

Build from source

JDK 17, Android SDK platform 35 and build tools 35 are required. Full instructions are in docs/BUILDING.md. 

git clone https://github.com/Cortex-AI-IDE/VS_downloader.git
cd VS_downloader
echo "sdk.dir=/path/to/android-sdk" > local.properties
export JAVA_HOME=/path/to/jdk-17
./gradlew test
./gradlew assembleDebug

The build produces one APK per CPU architecture under app/build/outputs/apk/debug/. Each is about 52 to 62 MB, because Python, yt-dlp and FFmpeg are bundled for that architecture only. The universal build, app-universal-debug.apk, carries all of them together and is about 151 MB.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people search for most.

What is Video Saver?

Video Saver is a free, open source Android application that downloads videos from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. You paste a link, tap Download, and the video is saved to the Download/VideoSaver folder as a standard MP4 file. It runs the yt-dlp engine inside the app, so no server or account is involved.

Which sites does the app support?

YouTube (including Shorts and live links), Instagram posts, reels and share links, Facebook videos, reels and fb.watch links, and TikTok video links including the short vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com share links. Because the engine is yt-dlp, most other sites that yt-dlp supports also work with a pasted link.

Why does TikTok say "couldn't decode, select another video" when I upload a downloaded file?

That message appears when the file is not a standard MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. Older versions of the downloader could fall back to WebM, MKV or AV1 files, which the TikTok uploader cannot read. Video Saver 1.2.0 and newer always prefer H.264 inside an MP4 container. Delete the old file, update the app, download again, and upload the new file. The troubleshooting guide explains how to check a file.

Is Video Saver free and open source?

Yes. It is released under the GNU General Public License version 3 and the full source code is on GitHub. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no paid features.

Does the app collect personal data?

No. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising library. The app does not ask for an account or a password. The only network traffic is the video download you request and the optional engine update.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer. The app targets Android 15 (API 35) and follows scoped storage rules, writing downloads through MediaStore into the public Download/VideoSaver folder.

Can it download private or age restricted videos?

No. Private, age restricted and login walled videos need cookies from a signed in browser session, and the app does not import browser cookies. Public videos download normally.

Is it available on Google Play or F-Droid?

Not on Google Play, because its policy does not allow apps that download media from other platforms. The app is distributed as a direct APK from the GitHub releases page. An F-Droid listing is planned.

Privacy and license

Video Saver has no telemetry and asks only for the permissions it needs: network access, notification permission for the progress bar, and storage access for the saved files list. The source is licensed under GPL-3.0. Third party components are listed in the NOTICE file.
